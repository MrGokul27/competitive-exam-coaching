"How should I manage daily current affairs notes without spending 3 to 4 hours on multiple newspapers?"

Senior Faculty Panel Advice
Academic Directorate
General Studies & Current Affairs Wing

Never copy entire news articles verbatim. The biggest trap is turning your notebook into a secondary newspaper. Here is the proven 3-step high-efficiency routine:

01
45-Min Editorial Scan

Read solely the lead editorial page for arguments and perspectives. Skip routine political or crime news.

02
GS Syllabus Tagging

File key data points, constitutional articles, and case laws under GS-I, II, III, or IV syllabus headers.

03
1-Page Monthly Capsule

Leverage our daily 1-page PDF summaries. Active weekly revision always beats voluminous manual transcription.
